Interactive Walkthrough: How One Shot Gets Decoded

You can't read the 4 logical qubits directly without collapsing them. Instead, 14 check qubits fire 70 binary detector alarms across 5 rounds. Pick a fault scenario below to trace how the cascade resolves it.

Tier 0: Zero-Check & Weight-1/2 LUT O(1) Hash

Packs 70 detector bits into 128-bit integers and checks an open-addressed table of single and top-K pair DEM faults.

Tier 1: Numba float32 Min-Sum BP 5 Iterations

Runs damped normalized min-sum message passing with per-thread pre-allocated workspaces; exits early if H·ê ≡ s (mod 2).

Tier 2: 128-bit Packed GF(2) OSD RREF + Sweep

Sorts DEM columns by BP reliability, runs bitwise GF(2) Gaussian elimination, and sweeps unreliable non-basis combinations.

Engineering Constraints & Honest Trade-Offs

Rule Compliance

  • Zero external QEC decoders (pymatching, ldpc, stimbposd, etc. verified absent via AST check).
  • Uses only numpy, scipy, numba, and stim with pinned versions.
  • 100% bitwise deterministic across runs with import-time Numba JIT warmup.

Known Limitations

  • Runs in ~50 s for 200k shots on a 1.2 GHz dual-core i3 laptop (above the 15.4 s judging target on 48 EPYC vCPUs).
  • Hyperparameters (ms_scale=0.78, damping=0.20, osd_w1=64, osd_w2=20) are hard-coded without full sensitivity plots.
  • Standalone competition artifact kept separate from Pushkar0997/qecscreen.
